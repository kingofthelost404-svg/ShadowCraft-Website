From the Nightmare's Edge

Development notes and site updates, lightweight enough to post as often as they happen.

August 1, 2026

Aspects, Memories & Creatures archives rebuilt

All 41 obtainable Aspects, all 110 registered Memories, and all 25 named Nightmare Creatures are now catalogued with real filters and search, replacing the old placeholder cards.

August 1, 2026

Flaws page retired

The Flaws archive has been removed. A player's Flaw is meant to be discovered in-game, not spoiled on a public wiki.

August 1, 2026

Site reskinned in the Nightmare Spell's own palette

The whole site now shares the void-black, gold, and purple look of the mod's in-game Spell UI, down to the pulsing corner runes and glowing titles.
