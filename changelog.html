From the Nightmare's Edge

Development notes and site updates, lightweight enough to post as often as they happen.

August 10, 2026

Aspects archive resynced with the mod

The Aspects archive now tracks the mod's registry: 64 obtainable Aspects, up from 41. Twenty-five new entries join the list, among them Nightingale, Graviton, Iron Sovereign, Red Wolf, Holy Templar, Blizzard Beast, The Jester and Dull. Bubble is listed at its real Dormant rank and Lifebringer as the Utility aspect it actually is. The archive also gains a Below Mundane rank and an Anti-Utility type for Wretch, the one Aspect that is a punishment rather than a power. Hatefor and Traveling have left the list: both are now off-ladder and no longer rolled.

August 1, 2026

Aspects, Memories & Creatures archives rebuilt

All 41 obtainable Aspects, all 110 registered Memories, and all 25 named Nightmare Creatures are now catalogued with real filters and search, replacing the old placeholder cards.

August 1, 2026

Flaws page retired

The Flaws archive has been removed. A player's Flaw is meant to be discovered in-game, not spoiled on a public wiki.

August 1, 2026

Site reskinned in the Nightmare Spell's own palette

The whole site now shares the void-black, gold, and purple look of the mod's in-game Spell UI, down to the pulsing corner runes and glowing titles.
