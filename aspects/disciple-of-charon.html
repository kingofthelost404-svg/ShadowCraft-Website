Disciple of Charon

Awakened Support Aspect Theme · Ferryman, Hoarder

A Support Aspect of the stygian boatman: leading souls over the river Styx was not just your job but also your destiny. A ferryman's kit, boatcraft and the paddle, a temper for unruly souls, and a hoarder's greed spent on allies.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Stygian Paddle Training

Passive

Years of poling the Styx have defined your body: boats you steer run 50% faster, and a held stick carries the paddle's true weight, knockback on every smack, and a launching riptide surge in water or rain. River-souls riding with you knit flesh quicker too.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Boatman's Temperament

Passive

Souls have resisted the call of death before, and sometimes you just have to beat them into shape. Striking the undead, or a bearer of a soul, darkness, or death Aspect, steels you with Strength II and Speed I for 5 seconds, and blows from those same sources are partly turned aside (25% less damage).

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Hoarder of Coin

Active

Your own hoard steels you, a passive buff to Strength and Speed that grows the richer you carry. Spend it: sacrifice valuables from your pack to buy Strength, Speed, and Regeneration for every ally within 8 blocks for 60 seconds, the more coin given up, the stronger the boon.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Charon's Will

Transcendent FormToggle

Become the ferryman entire: a giant golem of minted gold and gemstone. While it holds, the four coin attacks, Wave, Swipe, Boat, and Shield, are yours to command. Costs 40 essence to rise and drains 5 more every second; when your essence runs dry, the form collapses.

Coin Wave

Active

The golem's hoard bursts outward in a 6-block ring of flung coin, slowing everything it hits. Player victims are buried in gold nuggets, a pack too full spills at their feet, and one unlucky stack is knocked clean out of their hands. Crowd control and disruption, not damage, by design.

Coin Swipe

Active

A giant paddle of minted gold smacks once across the golem's front in a wide arc. Anyone caught in the swing is slowed and left bleeding, get hit by a slab of solid gold and you would bleed too, or at least have a concussion.

Coin Boat

Active

A small boat of coin rises out of the Styx at your mark, hangs for a breath, and comes crashing down in a burst of gold piles, heavy damage to everything caught beneath it when it falls.

Coin Shield

Active

Wrap the looked-at ally, or yourself, with none in sight, in a shell of coin that absorbs damage before it scatters. The ward stands protected for 30 seconds or until the shell is spent, whichever comes first.
