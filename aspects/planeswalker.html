Planeswalker

Sacred Utility Aspect Theme · Distance

A Sacred Utility Aspect of movement and distance. A road that gathers underfoot wherever you walk, fire immunity from the moment it takes hold, an out-of-body scout, a shortcut to remembered places, and a Transcendent form that scatters the bearer across the sky as walking stars.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Walking

Toggle

Walk on water and lava like solid ground: step off a ledge and the stars manifest a bridge beneath your feet as you walk, lingering roughly 20 seconds for anyone to cross. Jump to climb the path a step higher, or, falling with nothing beneath you, to catch yourself on it. Hold Shift to walk it back down. A quiet immunity to fire means the road ahead never burns you. At Ascended the road no longer needs ground to start from, and stays under you however high you climb, while the water stops drowning you and the dark stops hiding anything.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Spirit Walk

Toggle

Leave your body standing where you used it and cast your awareness across any distance to watch through another player's eyes, undetected. Use it again to be asked whether to come back or go and watch somebody else. Costs essence each second, and returns you to your body the moment it runs out.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Crossing

Active

Step across distance itself to a place you've lingered before.

Save Waypoint

Active

Manually mark where you stand as a Crossing waypoint, a remembered place you can step back to later.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Stars

Transcendent FormToggle

Scatter yourself across the sky as walking stars: your body disappears into the trail entirely, you fly freely, and you can hop between possessable star bodies at will.

Summon Star Clone

Active

Scatter another star into the sky, a possessable body you can hop into later.

Constellation

Active

Survey every star you've scattered and hop your consciousness into one, or return to yourself.
