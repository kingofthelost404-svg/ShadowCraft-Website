Rabbit Embodiment

Dormant Utility Aspect Theme · Avoiding Danger

A Utility Aspect of not being caught. You take on a rabbit's qualities, quick, twitchy, aware of everything around you, and every part of it is built around leaving before the thing that wants you arrives. The senses that keep you alive are the same ones that make you worth hunting.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Rabbit Qualities

Passive

You have a rabbit's nimbleness and a rabbit's nerves. You move markedly faster, clear a fence without breaking stride, and everything around you that might mean you harm stands out to your eyes alone.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Rabbit's Evasion

Passive

You are simply not where the blow lands. Incoming hits have a chance to be turned aside outright, but slipping one costs essence, and an empty soul cannot flinch.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Speedy Leaps

Active

Swift leaps in quick succession. Blink a short distance in the direction you are looking. You hold three leaps at once and they come back one at a time, so spending them all leaves you standing still for a long while.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Rabbit Form

Transcendent FormToggle

Stop pretending to be anything else. You shrink to a single block, move twice as fast, clear three blocks from standing, and no fall can touch you. Costs essence to hold.
