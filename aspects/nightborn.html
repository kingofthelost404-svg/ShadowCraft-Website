Nightborn

Transcendent Combat Aspect Theme · Blood

A Transcendent combat Aspect of blood and night. You are a creature of the night, feasting only on the blood of the living and fearing the harsh daylight.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Unlife

Passive

Your constitution is inverted into undeath. Hunger is gone: in its place is blood, a far deeper reserve that no mortal food will fill and that starvation can never kill you through. The dark holds nothing back from your eyes. But blood runs down, and a body running dry slows, weakens, and finally will not run at all. At Awakened the corpse settles further: the undead pass you by as one of their own, and water cannot drown lungs that no longer breathe.

Bite

Active

Hold the key on the body in front of you and drink. Blood crosses a mouthful at a time, and the victim bleeds by exactly as much as you took. Either of you moving pulls your fangs loose and puts them on a short cooldown. Come back to the same throat as often as you like, but a body you finally bleed dry is cold to you for a full day. The undead have nothing to give.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Hemomancy

Active

Pour the blood you have taken into a shape. Hold the key to choose between a ball that bursts, a dart that flies true, a spear conjured into your hand, and the exchange that pours it back into your own body, then tap to cast. Every shape costs blood as well as essence. Sneak while casting the exchange to trade every drop you hold.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Eternal Ascension

Passive

Time makes you harder to put down. Every day you live through strengthens your strikes, your stride and your body's mending, up to a limit no elder has passed. Dying takes every day of it back.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Wolf Form

Partial FormToggle

Take the shape of a sleek black wolf with red eyes: a keener bite and a great deal of ground covered, for a mild drain each second.

Giant Bat Form

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a giant winged predator: free flight, a boosted Health pool, blood that mends you as it holds, an unwielded spear cast, and Hemomancy at half the blood, for a heavier drain each second.

Winged Form

Toggle

Grow a pair of wings and glide, for a mild drain each second.
