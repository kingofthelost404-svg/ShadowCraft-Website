Lightbound

Awakened Support Aspect Theme · Light

A Support Aspect of Light: you carry light to bless those you choose to protect, bottled as curative draughts, condensed into shards and Beacons, forged into soul bonds, and finally become light itself.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Shared Blessing

Active

Gather the light around you into a curative draught, its strength shaped by what light there was to find, radiance brews deep mending, gloom barely any, true darkness none at all. Tap to bottle a draught anyone can use; once Ascended, hold to bless every bonded soul at any distance.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Lightforge

Active

Condense light into tangible shards and shape them into lasting constructs. Tap to condense a shard, stored light any ally can carry for minor protection against Shadow. Hold to combine your shards into a Beacon: a standing crystal that suppresses Shadow and quickens essence for those nearby, refillable with shards, and detonating in Divine light if overcharged.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Soul Reflection

Passive

Forge soul bonds that let trusted allies share strength and burdens. Bonds forge themselves through time spent fighting and traveling close together. Once forged: essence flows toward whoever needs it most, regeneration quickens, souls resonate when fighting side by side, and each feels the others' moments of danger. Three trusted bonds, eight once Transcendent.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Halflight

Partial FormToggle

The lighter, sustainable radiance: your body turns faintly translucent and gives off its own soft glow, your frame slighter, slipping through gaps that would normally stop you, lighting your surroundings as you move. Drains a little essence every second.

Unbound Light

Transcendent FormToggle

Become light. Pass clean through solid ground, stopped by nothing solid, intangible the whole time, and anyone you pass through takes a burst of Divine damage. Drains essence quickly.

Light Fragments

Active

Instead of remaining whole, split into fragments of light that attach to your nearby allies, each gains a weaker Halflight for a while: their own glow and a measure of protection. The split ends your Unbound Light. Only castable while it holds.
