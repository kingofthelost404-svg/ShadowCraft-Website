Weapons Maniac

Awakened Combat Aspect Theme · Weapon

A Combat Aspect of the armory: you hold all forms of weapons close to your heart, to the point of looking crazy to others. Read them, boost them, copy them, and never, ever betray the one you're bound to.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Weapons Proficiency

Active

Boost your combat efficiency while holding a weapon: +25% damage and +5% damage reduction for the window's length, but only while a weapon is actually in your hand.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Weapon Sense

Active

Read the weapon an enemy fights with: its true swing damage, every enchantment explained, and, for memory weapons, the memory's hidden lore. What you read stays Read for a while, their blows land softer on you, and yours land harder on them, the more their weapon out-classes your own. Read the weapon, not the wielder: they drop it or draw another, and the reading is worthless.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Weapon Duplication

Active

Manifest a throwable copy of the weapon you are holding. The copy flies where you aim and strikes the first living thing in its path for about half the original's damage.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Perfect Wielder

Transcendent FormToggle

Your body becomes perfect for wielding weapons: +50% damage, marked by a glinting spray of steel sparks. Drains essence every second.
