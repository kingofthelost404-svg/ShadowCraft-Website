Restaurant Manager

Awakened Support Aspect Theme · Food, Delivered Fast

A Support Aspect of food and getting it to people quickly. A manager tries his best to keep everyone and anyone fed, cook it out of nightmare flesh, deliver it across the field, warehouse it, and finally become the kitchen. Nothing this aspect does is for you alone.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Head Chef

Active

You cook real meals out of nightmare creature flesh, and everyone in your cohort eats twice as fast just by being near you. Flesh is worth points by the rank of what it came off, 5 for a Dormant creature, 5 more per rank, and the four meals are priced in those points.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Quick Delivery

Active

Hand whatever food you are holding straight into the packs of your cohort, nearest first, from right across the field. Anyone with no free slot misses this round; whatever you could not deliver stays in your hand.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Restaurant Food Storage

Active

Claim a single container as your storage, then reach into it from anywhere to pull food out. Each withdrawal costs sharply more essence than the last, and the price only cools off with time. You can never put anything in remotely, stocking the pantry means walking to it.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

The Walking Oven

Transcendent FormToggle

Become two blocks of cast iron permanently mid-bake. Far harder to hurt but slow, and your cohort no longer has to stop and eat, when a meal wears off, the next one is taken out of their own pack and used for them, and if they have run out, one is pulled from your storage into their hands. Drains essence every second.
