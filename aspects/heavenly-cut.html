Heavenly Cut

Transcendent Combat Aspect Theme · Wind, Tempest

A Transcendent Combat Aspect of Wind and Tempest: you carry the promise to cut the heavens, gathering Edge from every landed blow and spending it on blades sharp enough to bite through armor other weapons bounce off.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Gathering Wind

Active

Condenses a Windsword into your hands, absurdly sharp and free to create, but it shatters the instant it lands a real hit, granting Edge in return. Hold to throw it instead: guided at Awakened+, continuously re-aimed toward your crosshair at Ascended+.

Wind Compression

Active

Short bursts of compressed wind at your limbs and weapon: a dash out of a crouch into a sprint, added striking speed on a sneak-attack, extra force on your next swing after raising a weapon, and lighter footing when you launch off the ground sprinting. Each grants a little Edge, but needs constant control, chain them too fast and they cool down instead.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Lightning Temper

Active

Invest your entire current Edge into compressing your held weapon with lightning, the next hit it lands discharges the compression as bonus damage and a jolt of Lightning buildup. Hold to channel a brief electrical impulse through your own nerves instead, a short burst of reaction speed.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Storm Cut

Toggle

Toggle a Cutting Aura: countless shallow cuts tear at anything standing near you, while incoming attacks are gradually sliced apart before they land, and a ring of Windswords forms to orbit you. Hold to throw the nearest one at your aim. Edge maxing out primes your next successful cut to call down heavenly lightning, and Edge builds faster while the aura holds during a natural thunderstorm.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Squall

Partial FormToggle

A lesser Transcendent transformation, drawing on your essence: sharper reflexes and swing speed, lighter footing, cheaper Wind Compression bursts, and faster Edge generation.

Typhoon

Transcendent FormToggle

The full storm, drawing heavily on your essence: Windswords form and shatter on their own, your held weapon auto-compresses with lightning, the Cutting Aura's reach extends over the whole storm, and its cutting currents tear at everything inside it, friend and foe alike.
