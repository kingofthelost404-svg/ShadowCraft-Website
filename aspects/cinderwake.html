Cinderwake

Transcendent Utility Aspect Theme · Smoke and Ash

An Ascended utility Aspect of smoke and ash: thicken the air around you into a sensing veil, banking the embers it leaves behind as Ash to fuel what you shape from it.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Ashen Veil

Toggle

Thicken the air across a wide zone around you into a sensing haze of smoke and ash: everything living inside it is marked with a standing red thread, and you slowly bank Ash while it holds, at a steady essence cost. Hold to open the Marked list and spend banked Ash weakening or slowing anyone it names.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Smoke Mirage

Active

While the Ashen Veil holds, conjure a stationary illusion of smoke and ash within it, intangible, gone in a single hit, and paid for from both your Soul Essence and your banked Ash Reserve. Anyone who stares too long at one has their mind gnawed at and their eyes seized, unable to look away until the mirage finally unravels.

Command Mirages

Active

Open a list of your active Smoke Mirages to send one to a new position, overload it as a trap, or dismiss it.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Cinder Forge

Active

Shape banked Ash into a physical construct with a tap. Hold to open the Forge: shape a cinder sword, a cinder bomb, or a cinder cloak, or arm the nearest construct, mirage, or trap in reach.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Legion of Cinders

Transcendent FormToggle

Enlarge your body by consuming stored smoke, becoming immune to unenchanted physical attacks and drawing health and Ash from any smoldering ash nearby while it holds, at a steady cost in Soul Essence and Ash Reserve. Hold to instantly trade places with an active Smoke Mirage in range.
