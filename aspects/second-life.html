Second Life

Awakened Support Aspect Theme · Restoration / Decay

A Support Aspect of Restoration and Decay: nothing remains broken forever, and nothing remains pristine forever. Channel essence into a thing to walk it back toward what it was, or carry the rot on your strikes to walk it forward into ruin, and at the height of either, hold it there until it sets.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Restoration

Toggle

Channel essence into whatever you are looking at, or into your own gear, with nobody in your sights, and it slowly returns to what it was: armour, weapons, shields, tools, damaged Memories, and living bodies alike. Repairs come gradually over seconds, never in a burst. Nothing destroyed can be restored, and anything currently under attack mends far more slowly.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Corrosion

Toggle

Your strikes carry gradual decay. Every landed blow plants Decay in the victim and eats at their equipment; while you stay near them the rot keeps working, damaging them slowly and dulling any healing they receive. Blocks give way easier under your hands. One hit is deliberately weak, its strength is in a long fight, refreshed and stacked, until the decay consumes them outright.

Cold Weld

Active

Forces a shattering soul back together with no heat and no time to do it properly. Stops the break outright and leaves them at 1% Soul Stability. It holds. It was never going to be as strong as it was.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Reforge

Active

Push a thing past its ordinary limits instead of merely repairing or ruining it. Restore a subject to near-perfect condition, or pile enough Decay onto one, and you may set it there, granting it a temporary property. Only one Positive and one Negative Reforge may exist at a time; setting a new one lifts the old.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Eternal Bastion

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a towering construct of ancient metal and living stone, radiating restorative essence. Far more Health, real resistance, and immovable, but slow. While the bastion stands, Restoration reaches every nearby ally at once and Corrosion spreads from each corroded enemy to those beside them. Drains essence every second.
