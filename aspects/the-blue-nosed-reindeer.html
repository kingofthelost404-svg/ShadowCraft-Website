The Blue Nosed Reindeer

Dormant Combat Aspect Theme · Reindeer

A Combat Aspect of being, plainly, a reindeer: built for the cold, built to endure, and lit by a nose nothing else has. You are the one who keeps going when the weather stops everyone else, and the nose that lets your people follow you is the nose that makes you a target.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Fur Coat

Passive

You have grown a heavy coat of fur over your whole body. Cold takes hold of you far more slowly than it should and blows land softer against all that bulk, but heat takes hold of you far faster.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Reindeer Antlers

Active

Grow a rack and take it in hand. It gores deeply and leaves a wound that keeps bleeding, and it hits harder the further past Awakened you have come. Right-click with it to throw your head back and let the antlers carry you for a few seconds. The rack wears out after enough landed blows, and then you grow another.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Light of Hope

Active

Your nose flares into a lantern over your head. For as long as it burns, nothing the world can inflict takes hold of anyone in your cohort standing near you, and you yourself move and strike like something being chased.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

The Blue Nosed Reindeer

Transcendent FormToggle

Stop holding any of it back and simply become the animal: twice your size, a quarter more Health, and a quarter faster. Drains essence every second.
