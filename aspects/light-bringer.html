Light Bringer

Divine Divine Aspect

A Divine aspect of radiant soul-fire. You are a creature of light that was banished and doomed to exist in the darkness, you bring radiance and warmth wherever you go, but with it comes indescribable longing.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Innate Ability

Held from the moment the Aspect takes hold, outside the rank ladder. Divine Aspects only.

Sundered Blood

Passive

You can directly absorb a portion of the soul essence of any creature destroyed by your flames, as well as of any human. Kills made while your Soul Flame wreathes a weapon, and any slain player, yield soul fragments absorbed on the spot, with no shard left behind, and the essence comes with them. Something that stood as your equal gives back half of everything you can hold. Weaker prey gives back a fraction of that, and two ranks beneath you it gives nothing at all; something greater than you gives a little more.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Soul Flame

Toggle

Your soul burns with the purest of flames, one flame, divided however you choose. Split your soul's total fire (each formed core carries a seventh of it) between your Powers (the healing white flame), an Armor of light, your Weapon, and your Body's prowess. Every percent lit drains essence and feeds the pain of your Kindling. Press the bound key to ignite or extinguish; sneak-press to redivide the flame.

Healing Flames

Active

Hold the bound key to channel your Powers into the white flame's touch. The flame mends its own and nobody else. Aim at an ally and only they are healed, up to 10 blocks away, aim at anyone else and it finds nothing to reach for, and simply heals you instead. Aim at the ground and the healing bursts around you, split between every ally caught in it, including you, for less each but reaching further. Aim at nothing and it simply heals you; right-click while still aiming at nothing to burst it outward instead. Costs its own toll in the Kindling's pain, separate from the rest of the flame.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Flame Manipulation

Active

You can bend all fire to your will. Press the bound key to shape the fire in your hand, hold it to cycle through the shapes you keep, and sneak-press to choose them. With your Soul Flame out you can only move fire that already burns; light it and you can make your own from nothing, white and divine, at the price your Kindling always charges for creation. Whatever is already alight nearby is drawn on first, and costs you almost nothing. Anything your own white flame strikes is left burning; fire scavenged from the world only burns what fire always burns.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

No ability at this rank.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

No ability at this rank.
