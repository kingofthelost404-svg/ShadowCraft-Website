Darkness

Ascended Utility Aspect Theme · Darkness, Peace

An Ascended Utility Aspect of darkness and peace. In the dark you hold unrivaled dominance, able to dissolve into the black itself and drown the world in it, but step into the light and it tears through the darkness and your very being.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Darkborn

Toggle

You merge seamlessly with true darkness, unseen, untouchable, tiny and swift, unable to harm or be harmed. It costs nothing and holds as long as the dark does, but you cannot slip away mid-battle, and the light forces you back into form.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Dark Transmission

Active

Your authority over the darkness expands, reach out to the dark within your grasp. Spend half your Soul Essence to open a Zone of True Darkness (up to three), blinding all but you who stand within, then step between your zones across great distances.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Night's Serenity

Active

Manifest the peace of the darkness. A wave of calm sweeps 15 blocks around you, wiping every status affliction clean and settling the heat and cold to nothing. Hostile creatures lose the will to fight and players are barred from striking one another for ten seconds, each crowned with a black halo.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

True Darkness

Toggle

Drown the world in true darkness. A 10-block aura of absolute, sight-devouring black surrounds you, never touching you, while all caught within it drown in swiftly-rising Darkness. Holds for 4% of your maximum Soul Essence each second.

Six-Winged Angel of Darkness

Toggle

Unfurl into a four-block, six-winged angel of pure darkness, hooded, cloaked, crowned with circling black haloes, and free to take to the sky. All who lay eyes upon you drink in your darkness. Holds for 15 Soul Essence each second.
