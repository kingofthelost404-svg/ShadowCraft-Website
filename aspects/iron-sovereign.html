Iron Sovereign

Supreme Combat Aspect Theme · Conquest, Dominion

A Supreme Combat Aspect of conquest: every kill permanently hardens your body, a defeated foe can be destroyed or bound as a thrall, your mere presence weakens all who stand near you, and at your peak you tower as a colossus of steel and will.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Endless Slaughter

Passive

Every kill permanently hardens you, Health, Attack Damage, Armor Toughness, and Armor all grow along a curve that slows the stronger you become, never truly capping but always growing harder to push further.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Plague of Steel

Toggle

Finishing off another Sovereign-touched soul offers a choice: destroy them for the same growth an ordinary kill would grant, or bind them as a thrall, marked, weakened, and yours to recall. Enslaving places this on a long cooldown; destroying does not.

Dominion

Active

Survey everything you hold: rank, health, loyalty and standing orders. Command them, put them away, feed them Soul Shards to make them stronger, or set them free.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Dread Presence

Passive

Your mere presence becomes a weapon: everything living nearby is weakened for as long as they linger close, the pressure scaling with how much power you have accumulated. Unleash it outward in a short, punishing burst of mind-rending pressure.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Iron Incarnate

Partial FormToggle

Take on the Colossus's power without its shape. Denser, larger and immovable, everything Endless Slaughter has given you counts for more, and your presence weighs heavier, all without the full transformation's terrible size. Rank and slaughter add to this frame as well, on the same curve, a fraction of what they add to the Colossus, which is the whole point of wearing it instead.

Colossus

Transcendent FormToggle

Grow into a towering giant, far more resilient and far harder-hitting, with reach to match your new size. Still entirely yourself, just immense. How immense is earned: the higher your rank and the more Endless Slaughter has fed on, the greater the height you rise to, and where there is no room for all of it, you take what the room allows.
