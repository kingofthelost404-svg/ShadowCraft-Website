Regrowth

Awakened Support Aspect Theme · Re/Growing

A Support Aspect of Re/Growing: your body is that of an ever-regrowing piece of flesh, carved into healing for others, hardened into shields, deadly to touch, and finally bloomed into the axolotl that regrows everything.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Regrow

Active

Heal yourself or others through the use of your flesh. Wound yourself and spend essence to carve a piece of flesh, anyone who eats it heals half the Health it cost you. Carve as many as you like, but every carve slows your own regeneration for a while.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Growth Shield

Active

Protect yourself or others with your flesh. Consume a carved piece and essence to grow an extra-HP shield over whoever you're looking at, or yourself with no one in your sights. It cannot stack, but it can be grown on many people at once.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Overgrowth

Passive

Your body has grown too much, it has become deadly to touch. Every hit an enemy lands on you has a chance to plant creeping Poison in them, its bar filling until it activates, and filling faster the further beneath you they stand.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Axolotl

Transcendent FormToggle

Bloom into the axolotl: health and regeneration doubled, your carving no longer slows your regrowth, your poison fills much faster, and the water is home, you see through it and swim with ease. Little combat to it; the health makes up for it. Drains essence every second.
