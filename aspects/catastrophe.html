Catastrophe

Ascended Combat Aspect Theme · Disaster

Become a being of destruction, and pour vast, impartial chaos onto all who approach.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Micro-Calamity

Active

Loose a disaster in miniature. A restless tornado that drags in and flings whatever strays close, a rooted hurricane that grinds everything toward its eye and lets nothing burrow its way out, or a storm cell whose bolts pick their own victims and leave them reeling. Hold the key to choose the calamity; press to unleash it.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

The Forces of Nature

Active

Wield the raw elements one at a time: winds that carry your stride and your leaps, a fireball that cracks the earth where it lands, or a single bolt called down on whatever you are looking at. Hold the key to choose a force; press to release it.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Storm of Chaos

Active

Raise a storm over everything around you. All caught inside are torn from the ground and swept circling through the dark while the winds grind them down.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Transcendent form still in design. Nothing to document yet.
