Water-Horse

Awakened Support Aspect Theme · Ocean, Travelling

A Support Aspect of the ocean and of travelling: gallop across the waters of the Dream Realm, wash healing over your allies, and carry them on your back as the mythical hippocampus in its Transcendent form.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Mending

Active

Invoke the waters to heal over your allies, a smaller heal, in return for washing over everyone near you that you've designated as an ally.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Gallop

Active

Propel yourself with water to reach extreme speeds, launch in any direction, or directly upwards. While the gallop carries you, incoming blows have a chance to slide off, and any enemy you crash through takes a strike.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Water-Walker

Passive

The ocean is to you like land is to a horse. You do not sink on water unless you crouch, and you run faster whilst upon it.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Hippocampus

Transcendent FormToggle

Transform into the mythical water horse. Up to two players can right-click to ride on you, and you swim freely through the water at insane speeds which climb further as you sprint. Drains essence every second, faster if you stand still.
