Battery

Dormant Support Aspect Theme · Soul Essence

A Support Aspect of Soul Essence itself, you are the vessel that restores, stores and redistributes the power of souls. There is no attack anywhere in it; every tier is another way of moving essence from where it is to where it is needed.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Soul Recovery

Passive

Allied Souls near you refill faster than they should. The effect is strongest right beside you and fades to nothing at the edge, and where a stranger has to stand close, an ally you know is reached from much further off.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Essence Transfer

Active

Push your own essence directly into another soul. It has to be done at arm's length, and some of it is lost on the way, you redistribute power, you never make more of it.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Soul Reservoir

Active

Condense your essence into a stable crystalline sphere. How much it holds depends on your own capacity, not on what you had spare, and anyone at all can crack one open, so your spheres are a resource you can hand out before a fight.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Soul Tree

Transcendent FormToggle

Your body becomes a colossal tree of condensed essence, rooted to the spot and unable to walk. Roots reach every soul in the canopy: allies refill faster, enemies are drained and refill slower. The Tree ripens your own spheres into the world as fruit, and the third one anybody eats hexes them.
