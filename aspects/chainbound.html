Chainbound

Awakened Combat Aspect Theme · Chains

A Combat Aspect of Chains: a mysterious aspect that manifests spectral chains capable of restricting those who oppose you. Slow them, drag them, bind them, the chains never let go willingly.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Anchor

Active

Momentarily hinder a target's movement: a shackle of spectral chain on whatever you're looking at within 10 blocks, 35% slower for 2 seconds.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Chain Pull

Active

Manifest a spectral chain that drags a target toward you: launched up to 15 blocks, yanking the victim 5 blocks closer, 8 if they're Anchored, and leaving Slowness behind.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Chain Prison

Active

Summon spectral chains to bind a target in place: chains erupt from the ground and root them for 5 seconds, and when the chains snap, the freed target crawls out under a lingering slow.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Chain Hound

Transcendent FormToggle

Transform into an enormous black hound built of living chains, jaws of metallic fangs, broken shackles on every limb. 20% damage resistance, +15% speed, Anchor on every landed blow, and faster chains, with a long wait before the hound can rise again. Drains essence every second.
