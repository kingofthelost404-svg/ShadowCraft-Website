Dead

Supreme Combat Aspect Theme · Dying

A Supreme Combat Aspect of Dying: command death itself to punish your enemies.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Wound Expansion

Active

Tear a burst of true damage through whoever you're looking at, bypassing armor and reduction entirely. Refuses against no target or a target already at full health.

Wound Expansion

Passive

Every wound you deal risks widening: your landed hits carry a chance to plant Bleed in whoever you strike.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Forceful Puppeteering

Active

Hold to channel puppetization into whoever you're looking at while you're injured. Completing the channel binds them to your will, cancels if you're struck, or if the target dies, flees, or breaks your sightline.

Forceful Puppeteering

Passive

Every kill you land, or one of your puppets lands in your name, risks dragging the fallen back under your dominion as a new puppet.

Command the Dead

Active

Look over everything you have raised. Give it an order, put it away, call it back, or let it go.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Transference

Active

Hand your wounds and afflictions off to a nearby puppet: your current injury and every status buildup you carry pass onto it whole, leaving you restored. Should your own body ever be destroyed, a nearby puppet is sacrificed to spare you instead, free of charge.

Transference: Possession

Toggle

Trade places with a nearby puppet outright: your own body is left behind, vulnerable and unguarded, while you walk the world wearing the puppet's shape. Press again to return, your left-behind body's condition carries back onto you.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Blood Sea

Toggle

Call death itself over the ground around you: a wide aura in which everything caught takes steady damage, bleeds and rots, and any lesser creature is dragged unconditionally under your dominion as a new puppet. Drains essence every second.
