Groupy

Awakened Utility Aspect Theme · Teaming

A Utility Aspect of Teaming and Grouping, with yourself or others: become your own team. Friends splash into being at your side, fight for you, and when asked, go out with a bang.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

A Friend

Active

Summon a friend, someone who is you... but not! Spend half your current soul essence to splash a fighting Friend into being with your stats and your name. Your cohort doubles each rank counting yourself: one Friend, then three, then five, six of you in the room.

Say Goodbye

Active

Send your whole circle home at once. Costs nothing and waits on nothing, because it takes nothing away, the essence was spent when you called them, and you will spend it again when you want them back.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Sacrifice

Active

Sacrifice your friend to help you in battle. Your nearest Friend commits a watery explosion, massive damage to everything around it, and you healed a portion of your own health.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Together

Active

Bring yourself even closer with your friends, creating Friends of them too. Create a single Friend copied from the player you're looking at, their stats, their name, for half your own current essence.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

School of Fish

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a tiny fish with tens of copies swimming along with you, through the air and the sea alike, with quite a bit of speed. Individual fish can be flung at enemies to Sacrifice themselves. Drains essence every second.

Fling Fish

Active

Fling one fish of the school at whatever you're looking at, it Sacrifices itself, dealing damage and healing you slightly. Only castable while the school swims.
