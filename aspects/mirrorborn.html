Mirrorborn

Divine Divine Aspect

A Divine Utility Aspect of mirrors and reflection. Forge loyal Reflections from stolen Soul Cores, step through glass and still water and rain itself, steal the bodies of the unwary, and stash yourself away in a realm that exists only behind the glass.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Innate Ability

Held from the moment the Aspect takes hold, outside the rank ladder. Divine Aspects only.

Mirror Forging

Active

Spend stockpiled Soul Cores to shape a Reflection, a loyal construct fashioned in a form you choose, sent out to scout, decoy, fight, or carry in your stead.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Mirror Sight

Active

Tap to step whole into the reflection under your gaze, still water, glass, ice, polished metal, or any living creature's eyes, and watch the world from inside it. Your gaze does not have to be exact: reflections pull toward it, and a mark beside your crosshair shows what the next tap will take. Tap again to slide from reflection to reflection, or to turn round inside a pane and watch out of its other face. Sneak to step back out, and hold the key for your anchors, the reflections around you, and how far your aim reaches. Tap with nothing reflective in sight while the rain falls on you and you come apart into the storm itself, free to roam wherever the rain touches. Only breaking the surface, or a truly heavy blow finding your hidden body, can force you out.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Soul Invasion

Active

Fix your gaze on a living target and force your way into their soul, or reach for it from inside their very eyes while you dwell there. Win, and a person's body is yours to wear; win against a Nightmare Creature instead, and its soul bends to you, or its cores may be torn free one by one.

Consciousness Transfer

Active

Look in on everything your invasions have won. Step into any of the bodies you have taken and wield whatever Aspect it held in life alongside your own, send it where you please, or fold it away into your Mirror Realm until you need it.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Mirror Realm

Active

Cast through a reflective surface and the glass copies the ground it can see into a realm of your own, then steps you into it. Cast with an empty gaze and you step into the reflection as it already stands. Once inside, gaze into a reflection to step between your mirrored ground and your vault, where stashed bodies, vessels and belongings are kept. Hold the key instead of tapping it to look over everyone you are holding, and to let any of them go.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Mirror Split

Active

Shatter your presence across every Reflection you command at once, acting together, and swap which one is truly you at will. Lose a vessel and the strain lashes back at you, but you do not die while another still stands.

Mirror Legion

Active

Open the ground your enslaved creatures stand on. Every beast your Soul Invasion has taken whole is drawn where it truly is, and told what to do by pointing at the map: hold, heel, hunt, or hold one piece of ground together. Nothing here is summoned, an army of mirrors is gathered one won invasion at a time.
