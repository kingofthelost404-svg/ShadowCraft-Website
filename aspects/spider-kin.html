Spider Kin

Awakened Utility Aspect Theme · Spider

A Utility Aspect of spider descent: you inherited some of their traits. Sticky hands up any wall, a predator's eyes and venom, and webs of your own making.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Wall Climb

Toggle

Climb walls using your hands alone, made sticky by soul essence. Toggle it on, then leap at any wall and hold toward it to scale it, sneak to cling in place. Spends essence for every second spent climbing, stepping like a spider all the way.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Spider Traits

Active

Exhibit the spider's own traits for a while: night vision, and every landed hit plants venom, 2 points of poison buildup, growing 1 stronger with each ascension beyond Awakened.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Web Production

Active

Release webs from your hands sticky enough to trap a living being: a sheet of silk laid over the ground wherever you are looking, holding fast anything that walks into it before leaving them staggering, and rotting away half a minute later. The Spider form spins a wider sheet on a halved cooldown.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Spider Form

Transcendent FormToggle

Transform into a spider about twice a normal spider's size: +5% damage, +25% max health, +50% speed, free wall-climbing, and enhanced spinnerets. Drains essence every second.
