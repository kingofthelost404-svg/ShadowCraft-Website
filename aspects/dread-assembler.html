Dread Assembler

Ascended Combat Aspect Theme · Blood & Conjuration

Channel the grotesque rituals of an eldritch cult, sacrificing harvested stocks of life force to tear open reality and summon bloodbound thralls to overwhelm your enemies. A Combat Aspect of blood and conjuration. Nothing it does is free, and the price is always your own body or your own mind.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Harvester's Call

Passive

Slay foes to harvest stocks of vital essence, using their life force to empower your dark conjurations. Your melee blows fill the meter and a kill pays outright. What you kill also leaves pieces of itself behind, the stronger the thing, the better the piece, and the truly weak leave nothing at all.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Rite Of Passage

Active

Offer your stocks and your harvested parts at the altar to tear a demon into the world. Five parts may be set into the rite, and each one shapes what answers it. You cannot call anything greater than yourself.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Spawn Of Satan

Active

Spend harvested stock to mend the demons already in your service, or send one back where it came from.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Herald of the Flesh Horde

Transcendent FormToggle

Give everything you have to call one thing greater than all of it. Sorici answers, and Sorici answers to nobody, not even you. He walks for five minutes, and no bearer anywhere may call him again for two hours. Calling him takes you to the very brink of yourself.
