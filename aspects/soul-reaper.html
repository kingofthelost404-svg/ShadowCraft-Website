Soul Reaper

Supreme Combat Aspect Theme · Death / Soul

You are meant to reap. A Supreme Combat Aspect that feeds on death, severs the soul beneath physical defenses, grows a fractured core from what it breaks, and ultimately abandons the body as a Wraith.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Soul Feast

Passive

Killing a living creature tears free part of its soul and restores Soul Essence. Stronger prey feeds you more.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Soul Sever

Toggle

Toggle to sheath ordinary melee strikes in ghostly soul-light. Each fully charged hit spends Essence and damages the target's Soul HP directly, ignoring armor, toughness, shields, and physical reductions. Repeated blows can shatter the soul core and kill instantly.

Mist Blade

Toggle

Condense the chilling mist into a weapon, or let it dissolve. Costs no Essence, ever. Severing souls with the mist is free, a borrowed blade is not.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Shattered Core

Passive

Your fractured soul core absorbs remnants from slain living beings. Permanent Soul Mass raises it through five stages, increasing maximum Essence, physical power, speed, resilience, and knockback resistance without forming Divine extra cores.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Undying Slaughter

Transcendent FormToggle

Become an intangible specter of cold mist. Fly through solid matter and water, and let every physical blow pass through you. Nothing you strike is struck in the body: a wraith has no blade but itself, so every hit severs the soul outright, and it costs you nothing beyond the Essence the form is already burning.
