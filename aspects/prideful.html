Prideful

Transcendent Combat Aspect Theme · You

A Transcendent Combat Aspect of You: you are a god given gift upon the world, and all exist to bow at your feet. Your body and your power rise with the sun and fall with it, and you may never, ever lose.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Strongest

Passive

The sun is the measure of you. As it climbs you grow, taller, heavier of arm, longer of reach, quicker of foot, and your wounds close as fast as they open, until at noon you stand four blocks tall, strike half again as hard and shrug off near a third of everything aimed at you. As it sets you dwindle back to an ordinary person, and at night you are nothing more than that. Duck under a low ceiling and the world takes the height back, and the reach and the stride with it.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Sunshine

Passive

The day gives more, and takes less. Everything Strongest grants reaches further, and at the height of your power poison, wounds, madness and the rest simply fail to take hold of you. Corruption and Divinity are not affliction, and pay you no mind at all.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

The One

Active

Take hold of the sun and throw it. A lance of pure fire that costs a person a quarter of everything they have, and a beast a share of its own plus the full weight of your rank. Once a day, and no more.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Miniature Sun

Transcendent FormToggle

Call a small sun into being above your head. For five minutes you stand at the very peak of your power whatever the hour, and you are owed one throw of The One that the day cannot refuse, but you must not stray a hundred blocks from where it hangs. Once every seven days.
