Awakened Utility Aspect Theme · Resonance

An Awakened Utility Aspect of sonic waves, magnetic heat and cold, and radiant healing. Reads the world through vibration and current, and can travel at the speed of light in its Transcendent form.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Seismic Sense

Active

Send out a sonic pulse from where you stand, everything living caught in it glows for a while, revealing itself to everyone nearby.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Magnetic Manipulation

Active

Push everything around you toward whichever mode you've selected, hot, cold, or neutralize. Comfort and safety for you and nearby allies, real danger for any hostile mob caught in the same radius. Hold to choose.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Radiation Rajin

Active

Cast a healing pulse on whoever you're looking at, or on yourself if no one else is in your sights. The radiation always bites back at you either way.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Being of Light

Transcendent FormToggle

You collapse into a singularity, releasing a myriad of photons that boost essence and health regeneration on contact, allies and enemies alike. Tap the key again to hurl yourself as light, chain the bursts and you are flying; hold it to return to flesh. Drains essence every second.
