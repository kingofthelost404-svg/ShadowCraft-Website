Blizzard Beast

Transcendent Combat Aspect Theme · Patience, Coldness

Patience, and the coldness to wait for it. A Combat Aspect built around stillness, every ability holds its breath before it lands, and every debt it collects is paid to the enemy in frost rather than blood.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Freezing Feet

Active

Ice skating holds no secrets from you. Dash along the ground in the direction you are looking, chilling everything the passing frost touches.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Ice Heart

Toggle

As you blend in coldness, your heart freezes. Light your heart and every blow you land chills and slows whatever it strikes, for a minute at a time.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Polar Winds

Active

Your link with Ice is deeper, you feel yourself merging with the cold wind. Hold the key to choose the shape. Swords gather around you to be thrown one at a time, at no further cost. Spears tear up out of the ground on every side of you at once. The Bow is drawn slowly, and looses a single arrow that leaves whatever it finds easier to hurt.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Partial Form

Partial FormToggle

Half-human and half-beast, an arctic-fox tail grows from you, and your eyes take on the fox's own gleam. Faster on your feet, and the cold cannot touch you. Costs essence to hold.

Full Form

Transcendent FormToggle

Stop being a person holding an aspect. You become the beast itself, three-tailed, digitigrade, larger and stronger and faster than you were. Freezing Feet needs no rest to reuse, and Polar Winds strikes harder in your claws. Costs essence to hold.
