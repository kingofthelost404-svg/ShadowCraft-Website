Red Wolf

Transcendent Combat Aspect Theme · Aggression

In a dark forest there was a wolf, gone mad with hunger, attacking everything in sight, and the closer it came to dying, the harder it was to stop. A Combat Aspect of aggression. Everything it gives you is paid for in blood, and all of it goes quiet the moment you stop fighting.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Hunger

Passive

Blood fuels you, best to bleed the enemy dry before they do the same to you. After you are wounded, every blow you land claws back a piece of what it cost you. The debt fades over five seconds, so the only way out of a bad trade is to press it.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Bloodlust

Active

The fury of battle fills you with strength. Fighting builds bloodlust, more for blows landed than blows taken, and every point of it makes you hit harder and move faster. It bleeds away fast when you stop. Spend the whole meter to close your wounds over ten seconds, and go without for thirty.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Malice

Passive

Malice towards all life, forever enduring. Every creature you kill teaches you how to kill its kind, a permanent, growing edge against that species alone, up to a limit that lifts as you rise.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Red Wolf

Transcendent FormToggle

Stop being a person holding an aspect. You become the wolf from the story, and the nearer you are to death, the more terrible you hit. A healthy wolf is nothing special; a dying one is a catastrophe. Your hunger for blood also lingers twice as long. Costs essence to hold.
