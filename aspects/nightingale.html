Nightingale

Ascended Utility Aspect Theme · Sky and Authority

An Aspect of freedom, height, and impossible movement through open air.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Flight of the Nightwyrm

Passive

Grants survival flight while the ability remains unlocked. Staying in the air burns Essence every second, and the wings fold when it runs out. They fold too while you are in a fight with another Sleeper, and there is no striking with your hands while they are out.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Dragon Eyes

Toggle

See far and in darkness for free. Focus to look through walls and concealment, then hold one enemy in your gaze to read their strength. An analyzed enemy cannot hide its condition, and your attacks exploit the weaknesses you found for greater damage and armor penetration.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Dread Voice

Active

Your voice carries unquestionable authority. Speak, and everything in earshot answers, allies steadied and hurried, enemies halted, thrown back, made small or made silent. The more of yourself you put behind the word, the harder it is to refuse.

Speak Again

Active

Repeat your last order without stopping to think about it.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Midnight Dragon

Transcendent FormToggle

Stop being a person who can fly. You become a vast dragon in dark scale lit from within by starlight, faster in the air than anything alive, your voice carrying across a valley. You will not fit indoors. Costs essence to hold.

Sonic Roar

Active

Let the roar out as a weapon. A wide cone of pressure that hurts, throws, and breaks whatever anyone in it was concentrating on.

Dragonfire

Toggle

Breathe. A narrow, sustained stream of fire that engulfs everything in front of you and leaves it cooking. Paid for by the second, and it will not run forever.
