Depths

Awakened Utility Aspect Theme · Deep Sea

An Awakened Utility Aspect of the deep sea: channel the darkness only born from the depths. The ground is the bearer's ocean, they swim through it, chain their enemies in darkness, drag them under, and finally become the massive whale that lives below the world.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Submerge

Toggle

Travel through the ground as if it were your ocean, swim through walls, the floor, and the ceiling, wrapped in the deep's darkness. Drains essence every second; when it runs dry, the ocean closes and sets you back on solid ground. A blow landed on you by another Sleeper tears you out of the deep and shuts it for fifteen seconds, striking first costs you nothing, being struck costs you the ground.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Deep Chains

Active

Channel the depth's darkness to restrain your opponent. Chains of darkness bind whatever you're looking at in place, anything, even creatures made intangible through Shadows and the like.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Dark Depths

Active

Bring your enemies down into the depths of the world. A sinking curse drags your target under, Shadows cling to them, they suffocate slowly, and only constant rising against the pull can save them while you remain free to strike.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Whale

Transcendent FormToggle

Transform into a massive Whale. It cannot swim through the air, it swims through blocks, as if Submerge were permanently upon you. Drains essence every second.

Breach

Active

The submerged whale hurls itself into the air. Everything caught beneath it when it crashes back down takes the slam and is automatically bound in Deep Chains. Only castable in the Whale form.
