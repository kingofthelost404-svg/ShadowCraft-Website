Star

Awakened Combat Aspect Theme · Starlight

An Awakened Combat Aspect of starlight: become a blinding star. Starlight condenses into weapons, plants searing Overheat in everything it touches, and detonates it in devastating bursts once awakened.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Starlight Manipulation

Active

Manipulate the starlight into a multitude of forms, condense a sword, a spear, or a shield of pure light. Every swing of a condensed weapon plants Overheat in its victim. Tap to condense; tap again with a condensed weapon in hand to shift it to the next shape; hold to choose the next shape.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Starlight Burns

Passive

Empower the starlight on opponents to deal devastating damage. Whenever heat you planted bursts, starlight explodes out of the victim, massive damage, and Overheat spreads to everything nearby.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Starlight Passage

Toggle

Converge starlight to light your path, a hovering hold that locks you at your exact height. You can neither rise nor fall while it carries you. Drains essence every second.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Star

Transcendent FormToggle

Transform into a blinding Star: flight, a corona that constantly plants Overheat in everyone near you, and the power to send off bits of your body as blasts of light. Drains essence every second, faster by day.

Starlight Blast

Active

Loose a bit of your blazing body as an instant beam of light that burns and Overheats whatever it strikes. Only castable while the Star form holds.
