Valor

Transcendent Combat Aspect Theme · Living Metal

A Transcendent Aspect of metal, sharpness, and war. Your body becomes a living weapon: hard to break, ruinous to touch, and capable of engraving sacrificed powers into itself.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Sharpness of the Living Blade

Passive

Your body and wielded weapons radiate cutting force. Fully charged melee attacks carry an additional edge equal to 15% of your progressed maximum Health, and that edge grows with your soul. Direct strikes can split soft blocks such as logs, leaves, wool, cobwebs, and other fragile materials.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Tempered Steel Body

Passive

Your body hardens like forged steel: 25% less incoming physical damage, 35% knockback resistance, and 45% less Bleed buildup. Absorbed defensive engravings and liquid metal form can harden it further.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Absorb Enchantment

Toggle

Hold the bound ability to open three numbered engraving slots. Choose any slot, then click a supported Soul Sea Memory twice to consume it permanently and assimilate its registered property. Passive engravings work automatically; active engravings appear in the Soul Sea Ability list and can use any normal ability bind. Replacing a filled slot permanently destroys its old engraving.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Liquid Metal Form

Transcendent FormToggle

Toggle your Transcendent transformation into flowing metal. Activation costs essence, followed by rank-scaled upkeep each second. River form grants speed, knockback resistance, physical resilience, and a damaging sprint impact; sword form grants reach, a stronger Living Edge, and cleaves nearby enemies. While active, sneak and activate to switch modes. Sneak near dropped swords, metal tools, ingots, blocks, or anvils to absorb them as mass. Mass increases upkeep but buffers incoming physical damage.
