Knight of the Hearth

Awakened Combat Aspect Theme · Heat, Knighthood

A Combat Aspect of heat, knighthood, and temperature: the heat of the myth of divine flows through you, you shall never fear the cold again. A warming hug that gifts fire, honor that armors you, and the power to steal the very flame from the world.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Hestia's Embrace

Toggle

Embrace the surroundings in an ever-warming hug, bringing with it the gift of fire. Things randomly catch alight around you, food and beasts cook, and players who linger too long inside the warmth begin to burn with slow overheat. Drains essence while it burns.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Knights Honor

Passive

Thy honor has reached bounds no one else has reached before, revel in it. Grants protective blessings that grow with your tier: Resistance, then Absorption, then Regeneration.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Thief of Fire

Toggle

Steal the gift of fire from the surroundings, watch them freeze. Replaces the warming hug with a circle of stolen warmth: flames die, beasts freeze in place, and those who linger freeze solid, weaker each time it takes the same victim.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Hearth Knight

Transcendent FormToggle

Don black armour of still-burning charcoal, a hearth where your chest would be. Both circles may burn at once and stack, their reach doubles, and you may plant Torches of Fire and Ice. Drains essence every second.

Torch of the Hearth

Active

Plant a Torch of Fire, or of Ice, while the fire is stolen, at your feet. Left alone, its slow aura spreads outward, stacking with your own circles. Only castable in the Hearth Knight form.
