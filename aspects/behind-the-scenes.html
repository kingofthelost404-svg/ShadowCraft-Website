Behind the Scenes

Awakened Support Aspect Theme · Dirty Tricks

A Support Aspect of dirty tricks: use every trick in the book to get the advantage. Dread for whoever looks at you, quiet strength for your friends, and a way out when the knives come.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Intimidation

Active

For a short window, everyone within 10 blocks who looks at you is overwhelmed by their own traumas, Nausea II and Blindness II for as long as they keep staring. The window grows with every ascension.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Bond Creation

Active

Tie a bond to the player on your crosshair (cast again to release it). Bonded friends within 20 blocks of you quietly carry +15% damage and 10% damage reduction. The bond cap is 10 players, +5 per ascension.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Escape Artist

Active

When your health drops low under another player's blows, vanish to the side of a bonded friend within 300 blocks. A very long cooldown, one exit per act.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Kecleon

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a small green chameleon-like creature with a curled tail and large eyes. Its real power is Camouflage, cast it while transformed to disappear entirely.

Camouflage

Active

The Kecleon's trick: for a minute (+2 per ascension) you cannot be seen or heard, and no tracking aspect, ability, memory, or echo can find you. A very long cooldown.
