Iron Heart

Transcendent Utility Aspect Theme · Metal, Swords

A Transcendent Utility Aspect of metal and blade. Your weapons answer your will, swinging without a hand to guide them, shielding you when drawn close, and mending when held still. At Awakened you forge new weapons from raw iron and monster parts, each one a true Memory. At Ascended, a wounded creature can be unmade into a blade outright. At your peak you become the weapon itself, a storm of every blade you own or a titan of living iron.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Magnetism

Toggle

Command the metal in your blade. Push swings faster and harder, knocks enemies back, and draws blood. Pull attracts loose metal and turns crouching with your weapon into a guard. Attach mends forged Memories in your hand and Soul Sea. Tap to change modes, crouch and tap to release them.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Blacksmith

Active

Open your inner forge. Melt iron and monster remains together, choose a casting, and hammer out a real Memory weapon. Each weapon you forge of the same type improves your craft, your mastery carrying forward into finer and finer blades.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Equivalent Exchange

Active

Unmake a wounded creature into a weapon. The creature dissolves into raw metal and reforms as a Memory whose tier matches the creature's rank. The weapon type is whichever casting you have mastered most at the Blacksmith's forge.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Storm of Swords

Partial FormToggle

Every Memory weapon you own deploys as an autonomous flying blade and grants flight. Tap to dismiss the storm. Crouch and tap to spend half your Essence on a Barrier rune; sprint and tap to erase the aimed zone with Blinding Light.

Iron Giant

Transcendent FormToggle

Transform into a massive metal colossus. Immense reach, devastating strikes, and iron armor, but ponderous. The ground shakes where you walk.
