Lumen Keeper

Dormant Support Aspect Theme · Steady Light

A Support Aspect of steady, shared light. Nothing it does can strike a blow, every tier is a small standing ward that costs almost nothing to keep lit, and asks nothing of anyone standing near it except to stay close.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Faint Ember

Toggle

Light a small ring at your feet. You and every ally standing in it move faster, and the first hit anyone inside takes is softened. Any hit that lands on you snuffs it instantly and locks it out for a few seconds, this is a ward for standing still, not for a fight already underway.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Holding Light

Active

Plant a wide, stationary zone of light where you stand. Everyone allied inside it heals faster for less hunger, and shrugs off some of what Poison and bleeding would otherwise build in them. It holds for a while on its own, or breaks early if you wander too far from its center.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Shared Radiance

Active

Mark yourself and up to two of the nearest allies with a standing glow. Every kind of harm lands a little softer on the marked, everything except what reaches the mind, the divine, or the soul directly, knockback barely moves you, and a little of what ails you burns away every second the mark holds.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

First Flame

Transcendent FormToggle

Stop being only a person holding a light. Your body goes half-translucent and takes on a warm cast, and everyone allied close around you heals faster, shrugs off debuffs quicker, and steadies against being pushed off balance, though the light slows them, too. You alone pay for it: the dark reaches you more easily while it burns, and letting it go rather than being drained dry keeps it out of your reach for a long while after.
