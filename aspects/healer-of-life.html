Healer of Life

Awakened Support Aspect Theme · Healing

An Awakened Support Aspect: you are chosen by healing. Every ability moves Health from one place to another, into an ally, out of your own body, or indiscriminately into anything standing in the wrong (or right) place.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Healing Touch

Active

Lock an indefinite regen target: whoever you're looking at, or yourself while sneaking. While locked, they're healed a small amount every second, draining your essence. Press again on the same target to release them.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Sacrificial Lamb

Active

Transfer a chunk of your own Health directly into whoever you're looking at, in one shot. Never drops you below 1 Health.

Mend the Core

Active

Knits a cracked soul back together. Restores 5% Soul Stability to whoever is under your crosshair, or to yourself if nobody is. It cannot touch what a soul has lost for good, only what it can still get back.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Zone of Health

Active

Plant a zone of healing where you're looking (or at your own feet, if nothing's in range). For its duration, it heals everything inside it, allies and hostiles alike, indiscriminately.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Great Butterfly

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a great, radiant butterfly: you grow, take flight, and every heal you perform is dramatically stronger while it holds, at the cost of essence drained every second.
