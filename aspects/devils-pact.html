Devil's Pact

Divine Divine Aspect

A Divine aspect of infernal transformation. Bargain a sliver of sanity for monstrous power, the more the mind frays, the deadlier the bearer becomes.

This Aspect predates the current Aspect template and is awaiting a rework. Bearers who already carry it keep it, but it is out of the roll pool until then.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Innate Ability

Held from the moment the Aspect takes hold, outside the rank ladder. Divine Aspects only.

Devil Form

Toggle

Assume a towering devil's shape: three blocks tall, twice the Health, wreathed in flame and shadow-wings. All around you, and any who dare watch from afar, drown in mind and death, but the transformation gnaws at your own mind while it holds.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Insanity

Passive

The frayed mind strikes harder and flinches less. At 25% mind: +25% damage, −10% physical taken. At 60%: +40% and −15%. At 80%: +45%, −20%, and every blow corrupts what it touches.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

No ability at this rank.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

No ability at this rank.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

No ability at this rank.
