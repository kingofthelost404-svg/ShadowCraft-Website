The Jester

Ascended Utility Aspect Theme · Falsehood

The Joker in a deck of a million cards. You can trick the people they call masters of their craft, and, in the end, yourself.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Heart of the Cards

Active

You no longer need to look at your hand. Five cards leave your sleeve in a fan, each hiding one of five afflictions, and not even you know which until it lands. Only one can take hold of a victim at a time.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Jester's Waltz

Active

You dance, and the dust comes up with you. Anyone who walks into the cloud is swallowed by the dark until they walk back out. Anyone but you.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

The Circus of Fools

Active

Everyone in the ring stops being able to tell each other apart, and the ones who came to fight start swinging at whoever is nearest. Anybody who never stepped inside sees it all exactly as it is.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

The Joker Incarnate

Partial FormToggle

The hat, the boots, the title. Your tricks come back to you whenever they feel like it, anywhere from instantly to five long minutes, and never the same wait twice.

The Last Laugh

Transcendent FormToggle

The jokes turn on you. A scythe, a second wave of cards, a wider dance, and your own mind going with all of it, a little more every second, until there is nothing left to laugh with.
