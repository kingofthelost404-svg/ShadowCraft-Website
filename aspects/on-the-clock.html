On the Clock

Ascended Utility Aspect Theme · Time

An Ascended Utility Aspect of Time. A bearer-only Time Gauge ticks away in the background, spent by every ability, refilled by the passage of time and by kills, and hitting exactly zero is instant death.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Rapid Acceleration

Toggle

Toggle a ramping burst of speed: faster movement and swings the longer it holds, draining both Time and Essence every second.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Deceleration

Active

Spend Time and Essence to slow whoever you're looking at: their movement and swings both lag for a time.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Partial Stoppage

Active

Spend Time to skip yourself forward along your sightline, the further the leap, the more it costs.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Silver Streak

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a blur: momentum builds while you move, speeding you up further, and every melee strike leaves after-image echoes that strike again a heartbeat later. Drains Essence every second while it holds.
