Pitiful

Dormant Combat Aspect Theme · Suffering Builds Character

A Combat Aspect of Suffering Builds Character: your life is a difficult one, the only possible reward is relief at its conclusion.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Human's Resolve

Passive

Grit your teeth and bear the pain, maybe you'll survive. A hit that would kill you instead leaves you at 1 Health, drains all your essence, and stuns you for a moment, but only once in a long while.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Forceful Swing

Active

Put your all into this desperate attack. The next swing does more damage; landing it leaves you weaker and slower for a time afterward.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Desperate Measure

Active

Offer your blood to the gods, they won't respond. Instantly triggers blood loss, but leaves you faster for a time.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Weakness Manifested

Transcendent FormToggle

You become half a block shorter and hit harder, and your weakness manifests as a dormant human wearing your own face. If it dies, or strays too far from you, so do you.
