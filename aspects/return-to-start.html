Ascended Support Aspect Theme · Unchanging

A Support Aspect of the Unchanging: you often find yourself starting things over again from the beginning. Wind a living thing's body back seven seconds, its health, its afflictions, even its soul and stomach, restoring exactly what was, for better or worse.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Return to Body

Active

Turn back the clock on a living thing's body by about 7 seconds, undoing what had just been done to it. A literal rewind of their health, a fresh wound closes, and health only just gained is unwound right back out. Castable on others or yourself.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Return to Status

Active

Turn back the clock on a living thing's status effects by about 7 seconds. A debuff just cleansed comes back; a buff just gained vanishes; a poison they didn't yet carry is unwound out of them. Castable on others or yourself.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Return to Soul

Active

Turn back the clock on a living thing's soul and stomach by about 7 seconds, then, for 70 seconds, their saturation is held at no less than a tenth of their max health. Castable on others or yourself, with a very long cooldown.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Prime Form

Transcendent FormToggle

Become the form of yourself you consider to be your prime, marked by a faded-gold aura of unwound time: +5% damage, +25% max health, +25% speed. Drains essence every second.
