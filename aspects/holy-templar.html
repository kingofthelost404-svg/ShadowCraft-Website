Holy Templar

Ascended Combat Aspect Theme · Holy Warrior

A holy warrior's fire, turned wholly against what should not exist. A Combat Aspect built to hunt corruption and nothing else, every ember it lights, every ward it raises, and every bolt it throws answers only to what has already shown itself hostile.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Purging Flame

Toggle

Light your weapon and swing it burning. The flame hits harder while it holds, but it drinks your essence and climbs toward its own overheat, burn too long and it dies, and the weapon must cool before you can light it again.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Heavenly Aura

Passive

A ring of light stands guard around you at all times. Corrupted creatures glow through walls at long range, death takes slower hold of your own soul, and fear and confusion pass through you faster than they should.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Smite

Active

Loose a ball of light at your enemy. It passes clean through anything that is not corrupted and detonates the instant it finds something that is, burning every corrupted creature caught in the burst, nothing else feels it.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Templar Ascendant

Transcendent FormToggle

Stop being a person wearing holy fire. Rise as the templar the story remembers, taller, heavier-armored, and hitting harder in every way a body can. Every blow you land while formed drives another nail into whatever corruption still stands against you.
