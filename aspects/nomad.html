Nomad

Awakened Utility Aspect Theme · Cold, Traveling, Survival

A Utility Aspect of cold, traveling, and survival: you have lived a life of pain and fled from all that surrounds you, now you wander the cold of a world that has forgotten you exist. Show them you still exist.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Nomadic Breath

Active

You alone have wandered more than any other: your stride is longer, travel barely wears on your stomach, and every stretch of road restores your essence. Cast to freeze the water of your breath into an ice sword whose every hit builds Frostbite.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Scavenger

Passive

You have learned to use all that you can get and more, always on, never off: everything hits harder in your hands (junk most of all), and every item you pick up is stamped with a random Bleed, Overheat, Frostbite, or Poison rider that keeps working in anyone's hands.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Return to No Man's Land

Active

Summon a towering gate to the place no one else ever wanted to go. No one can pass it, but its cold leaks out across a hundred blocks, a Frostbite chill that ramps from a whisper toward a storm the longer the gate stands.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Woolly Mammoth

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a giant tusked woolly mammoth with hide thick enough to shrug off almost anything when you brace (sneak). While you move, the surroundings themselves are torn loose and flung at your enemies for fixed hearts of damage. Drains essence every second.
