Raised by Wolves

Transcendent Combat Aspect Theme · Ferocity, Endurance

A Transcendent Combat Aspect built entirely from the body: a wolf-pack childhood hardened into permanent Strength, Speed, Agility, Resilience, and Endurance, a durability package that turns your skin into armor, a presence that rallies every ally who can see you into a fighting stance, and at your peak the giant steel-warrior War Maiden.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Born Hunter

Passive

A wolf-pack childhood permanently hardened into your body: Strength, Speed, Agility, Resilience, and Endurance, always in effect.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Skin of Steel

Passive

Your hide toughens further, heavy Armor, resistance to every kind of incoming harm, and a sturdier stand against explosions.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

War Standard

Passive

Your presence alone rallies the fight, every ally who can see you and stands near you while embattled fights harder, faster, and steadier for as long as they stay close.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

War Maiden

Transcendent FormToggle

Rise as a towering giant clad in steel. A war-horn blast disorients everything hostile nearby the instant you rise; every empty-handed strike after that unleashes a shockwave, or, sneaking, a wider stomp that sends enemies flying.
