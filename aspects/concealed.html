Concealed

Ascended Utility Aspect Theme · Concealment

An Ascended utility Aspect of concealment and darkness: become one with the dark, hiding your intentions and actions as a hidden threat, but avoid frontal combat and prolonged exposure, for they place you at a great disadvantage.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Partial Concealment

Passive

An incomplete concealment: you are truly hidden, unseen and beneath the notice of every hostile, while you do not attack, take damage, or stand in someone's direct line of sight. Your steps are also permanently swifter.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Complete Concealment

Toggle

Rid yourself of the partial veil's flaws: while this holds you stay hidden even when struck or watched, draining a share of your maximum essence each second, but you cannot attack at all. Costs nothing inside your Realm of Concealment.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Realm of Concealment

Active

Spend most of your essence to drag every nearby player with you into a sealed realm of darkness, isolated from the outside world, until its time runs out or you choose to leave. Complete Concealment costs nothing inside.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Angel of Darkness

Transcendent FormToggle

Transform into a winged angel of pure darkness: free flight and sustained strength on umbral wings, draining essence every second it holds.

Darkness Authority

Toggle

Darkness blesses you: harder strikes and faster recovery in low light, bonus shadow damage on every weapon hit, and immunity to shadow damage. Toggle to drain the light from your surroundings, smothering everything nearby in darkness at a steady essence cost.
