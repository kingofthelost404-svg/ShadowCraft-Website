The Script Writer

Sacred Utility Aspect Theme · Plays

Fate is a manuscript, and you hold the pen. Write a name into your Book and the world obliges - but an author never walks onto his own stage.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

[Book of Fate]

Active

Open your Book and write a line about someone, anywhere in the world. You need only their name - but the more of them you know, the harder fate lands.

[Audience of Fate]

Passive

The more of other people's fate you command, the grander the act you can stage. Each basic fate written about someone new grants a stack. Fill the bar and fate will refuse your death once - at a terrible price.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

[Overwrite]

Active

A string circles you, learning. After the same attack has struck you seven times, you may hold it ready - and the next one is simply written out, for fate. Corruption can never be caught.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

[Grand Stage Descent]

Active

The theatre descends on the world. Which play runs depends on the fate in your hands, and everyone nearby is in the audience whether they came or not.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

[Curtain of Fate]

Partial FormToggle

A curtain of strings settles on your shoulders. It drinks two fate a minute and gives back a tenth more life and a swifter soul. Wear it long enough and you learn what lies past it.

[Guide of Fate]

Transcendent FormToggle

You stop being a man in a theatre and become the strings. Fate pays you instead of charging you, your works cost half, hostile powers turn back on the ones who cast them, and blows keep finding nobody there.
