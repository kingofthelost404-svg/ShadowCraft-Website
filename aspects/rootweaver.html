Rootweaver

Ascended Utility Aspect Theme · Genetic Change

You read living things the way other people read writing, and you cannot help correcting them. It starts with seeds, a handful of wheat becomes a wall you can eat, a sapling becomes cover that grows where you tell it. Later it is meat, then it is people. Everything you touch comes out of your hands as something else.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Genetic Change

Active

Hold any seed, sapling or cutting and right-click the open air to rewrite what it grows into, no key needed, though it can be bound like any other menu. Planting the seed normally still works. A Natural Wall seed raises a wall of the plant's own material, leaves from a sapling, hay from grain, the gourd itself from melon and pumpkin, sized to your rank. A Support seed becomes a flower that heals whoever plants it, scaled to their own pool. Grain and gourd rewrites cost more, because a hay wall is also a week of food.

Spread the Growth

Active

Bind it, aim at another player and press: one stack of the cancer leaves you and two take root in them, because it is worse in a body it did not start in. Thirty seconds before that same person can take more, but you can turn straight to the next. One growth always stays behind in you, and starts climbing again tomorrow.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Bacteria meliorationis

Active

Culture a bacterium that improves your work. It does nothing on its own, drop it onto a rewritten seed in your pack, or the seed onto it, and that seed grows up: a Natural Wall becomes a closed dome, a Support flower becomes a whole field of them, each one healing whoever stands on it.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Fruit Synthesis

Active

Nightmare creature flesh is still living tissue, and living tissue can be told to be something else. Hold a piece and sneak-right-click to grow it into one of four fruits, plain right-click still just eats it. The fruit comes out at the rung of the carcass it was grown from: flight, a short hop, a tenth of your essence back, or a tenth of your Health. You can only carry so many at once, and they all count against the same limit.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Helix

Transcendent FormToggle

Come apart into what you are actually made of, RNA, DNA, and the machinery that reads them. In this state you can rewrite other people: an ally who consents runs faster, hits harder and recovers quicker, and a nightmare creature you touch is sabotaged for the rest of its life. The form grants you nothing. Everything it is for points at somebody else.

Human

Active

In Helix Form, aim at an ally and press to offer them two minutes of faster essence recovery, faster movement and harder hits, aim at nothing to rewrite yourself. They have to agree to it. Doing it to the same person twice before your hold on them fades starts turning them into something that is no longer a person.

Genetic Sabotage

Active

In Helix Form, aim at a nightmare creature and press. It hits softer and takes more, permanently. Edits compound, read the same creature again on a one-minute cooldown to spoil it further, up to a hard limit. Something exactly one rank above you takes only a token edit, and anything further above that you cannot read at all.
