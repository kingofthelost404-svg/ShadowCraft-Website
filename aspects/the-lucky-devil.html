The Lucky Devil

Awakened Utility Aspect Theme · Chance

Your soul is intertwined with the concept of chance. Every meaningful action becomes a wager, and fortune tips your way, provided you are willing to risk an equally devastating loss. The longer you lose, the better your next odds; the moment you win, the house settles up and you start over.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Loaded Dice

Active

Declare a Wager on your strength, your speed, your sight, your body, your next blow, or the next blow aimed at you. Win and it is significantly enhanced; lose and it is significantly hindered. Tap to place the same bet again, hold to choose a different one.

Lucky

Passive

Fate leans further your way with every rank you climb. Each wager you lose weights the next one more heavily in your favour, up to a hard ceiling, and the moment you win, the debt is paid and the odds fall back to whatever your soul alone is worth. Certainty is the one thing the coin will never give you.

Later known as [Fixed Game]

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Double or Nothing

Active

Having just won, let it ride. Win again and everything on the table doubles. Lose and you keep nothing, and the deeper the run you were on, the harder the fall.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Raise the Stakes

Active

Drag someone nearby into a wager with you, and set the terms yourself. Whoever loses pays, their weapon, their whole hotbar, or one of their powers taken from them for half a minute. This one coin is never weighted: rank, streak and The House all stay out of it, so it is exactly as likely to be you.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

The House

Transcendent FormToggle

Become the elegant gambler in white and gold, and stop betting against the odds. For forty seconds your luck climbs steadily, everything you win and everything you lose lasts longer, there is no limit on how far you can let a run ride, and your powers return twice as fast, but every failed wager cuts twice as deep.
