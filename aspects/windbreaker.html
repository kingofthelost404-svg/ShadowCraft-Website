Windbreaker

Awakened Combat Aspect Theme · Speedblitz, Ranger

A Combat Aspect of the rushing wind: ride like the wind. A speedblitz ranger's kit, close the gap in a burst, cut and bind from afar, and clap every foe out of your space.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Wind Dash

Active

Dash forward and strike your foes: a fast forward lunge on torn air. Everything caught along the path takes light damage, it is a long lunge, not a heavy blow. The Breeze form's winds carry it twice as far.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Blade of Winds

Active

Cut from afar: a long-ranged slice of compressed air at whatever you are looking at. A landed cut binds the victim in place for a moment. While the Breeze form's winds obey, the blade turns lethal, bonus damage and a bleeding wound.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Shockwave

Active

Walker of the invisible plane: a violent outward clap of air in a 5x5 footprint that expels every foe around you and flings them into the sky. A zoning tool built to combo with the rest of the kit, its damage stays light.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Breeze Form

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a creature of living wind, modeled after a Breeze. Wind Dash reaches twice as far and Blade of Winds turns lethal, but as punishment, the winds leave your control for the first 40 seconds of every transformation. Drains essence every second.
