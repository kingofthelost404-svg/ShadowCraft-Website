The Last Colossi

Cursed Combat Aspect Theme · Inversion

You are the last living thing of a kind the world has already finished with. It has no more use for you, and you have none for its rules: harm feeds you, mercy wounds you, and the wounds you leave only ever get worse.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Abyssal Lord's Blessing

Toggle

Turn the world the wrong way round. Blows that land on you close your wounds instead of opening them, and any kindness offered to you cuts. It costs you to hold, and everything you swing lands softer while you do.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Shadow Wolves' Strike

Active

The shadow still respects what you were. Something black runs out of it, bites once, and leaves the wound to do the rest.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Sky Clear Conquest

Active

There is no storm coming. Hold to choose what the clear sky does next; tap to make it do so.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Golden Scales

Partial FormToggle

The wings your kind was buried with, in gold. They will not hold you up, but they will carry you a long way down, and essence comes easier while they are out.
