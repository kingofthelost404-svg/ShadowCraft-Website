Bubble

Dormant Utility Aspect Theme · The Sea

A Utility Aspect of the membrane: a bubble is a wall, and a wall decides who is inside it. A sealed sphere nothing can cross, a bubble that carries one enemy out of the fight entirely, and a gun that shoves or mends but never wounds. Nothing on this ladder deals a single point of damage.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Personal Bubble

Active

Blow a great sphere around where you stand. For ten seconds nothing outside may enter it or reach through it, and nothing inside may reach out, not a blow, not an arrow, not a beast digging under it. Anything hostile caught inside when it goes up is thrown clear. Your own cohort come and go as they please.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Bubble of Exile

Active

Wrap whatever you are aiming at in a bubble and send it a very long way up. It drifts back down slowly, unhurt, and a minute later it is somewhere else entirely. Works on anything that can be aimed at.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Bubble Gun

Active

Conjure a gun that fires bubbles, and press again to change what they do. Blast bubbles burst on impact and hurl everything nearby away without hurting any of it; Healing bubbles mend the one thing they touch. One barrel has to cool before the other will fire.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Bubbleman

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a living bubble: incredible durability, no fall damage, and fast swimming, at the cost of essence drained every second. Sneak while transformed to pop yourself for a launching impulse.
