Arrow That Knows

Ascended Combat Aspect Theme · Unerring Sight

A warrior-seer Aspect. Its shots do not miss, but the only thing it guarantees is hitting. Power comes from preparation and information.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Unerring Aim

Passive

A shot loosed at a living creature near your crosshair cannot miss, the projectile knows its target from the moment it leaves you. The guarantee reaches 16 blocks as a Dreamer and grows with every rank, to 64 at Divine. A bow must be drawn to full strength for the Spell to guide it; a hurried half-draw flies wild. Silenced entirely while your Blind Spot is exposed.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Arsenal

Active

Pour soul essence into any held weapon, memory, or arrow stack. While the imbuement lasts, every hit dealt with it strikes harder, wreathed in essence, the bonus grows with the wielder's own rank, from a fifth of the hit as a Dreamer to four-fifths at Divine. Essence is spent at the blessing table; the hits are free.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Seer's Mark

Active

Sneak and hold your aim on a living creature to mark it: it glows, your ranged hits strike true for bonus critical damage, and it betrays its attacks a breath before they land. One mark at a time, ~20s duration, ~30s cooldown from expiry.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Transcendent form still in design. Nothing to document yet.
