Little King

Transcendent Utility Aspect Theme · Body Control

A Transcendent Utility Aspect of body control: your soul and body work differently from everyone else's. Go perfectly still and untouchable, cut the air itself into slashing gusts, bank your body's condition against the moment you need it most, and become an intangible gray haze.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Still Body

Active

Hold to go perfectly, unnaturally still, true immunity to any harm for as long as you hold it. The first two seconds are free. After that the stillness begins eating you, a tenth of your body a second, and it does not stop short of the end: hold it the full twelve seconds and it takes all of you. Whatever you have left when you let go, the longer you held it the longer you need before you can hold it again.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Wind Slash

Active

Cut the air into a slashing gust. Strike again twice more, within moments each time, to loose a final, far larger slash finishing the combo.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Life Rewind

Active

Bank your body's current condition. Trigger it again within the window to snap your Health back to that banked state, if it would actually help you, otherwise the moment simply passes.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Become a Gray Haze

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a gray haze for a few minutes: Still Body costs and drains nothing while hazed, and every other Little King ability's cooldown is halved.
