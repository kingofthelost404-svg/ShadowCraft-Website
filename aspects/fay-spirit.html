Fay Spirit

Ascended Support Aspect Theme · Fae Light

An Ascended support Aspect of fae light and song: auras that quicken allies' souls, conjured pixie dusts, shields of light, and cleansing pillars, all dimmer when turned on yourself.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Soothing Tune

Toggle

Toggle a melody around you: everyone inside the circle regenerates soul essence faster. Wider and stronger in the miniature form.

Fay Wanderer

Passive

Mobs are prone to attack you far less, most simply never take notice.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Pixie Dust

Active

Conjure a dust of the selected kind, three hexes for enemies, two blessings for allies, and use it on a creature. Sneak-activate to cycle the selection. Never works on yourself.

Pixie Heart

Passive

Your heart beats with fae vitality, steadily regenerating Health, quicker among trees.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Wall of Light

Active

Form a shield of light around yourself that absorbs damage before your Health, scaling with your rank. Thicker and longer-lived in the miniature form.

Being of the Forest

Passive

Among trees your soul essence returns faster (up to five trees) and your Pixie Heart beats half again as quick.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Miniature Form

Transcendent FormToggle

Shrink into a smaller version of yourself. While it holds, every Fay ability is upgraded, at a steady essence drain.

Bringer of Light

Active

Bring down a golden pillar that partially cleanses every ally in the circle of debuffs, bleed, and temperature extremes. Wider and deeper in the miniature form.
