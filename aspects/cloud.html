Cloud

Awakened Utility Aspect Theme · Weather

An Awakened Utility Aspect of weather and altitude. Calls a cloud to ride, turns storm and frost against whoever comes close, and in its Transcendent form stops being solid at all. Underground, it is nearly powerless.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Summon Cloud

Active

Calls a rideable cloud underfoot and mounts it. Costs essence every second while ridden, and disperses if left behind. Its speed climbs steeply with your rank, 12 blocks per second at Dreamer, 25 by Ascended, up to 50 at Divine. From Ascended it carries three riders instead of one. Cast again to dismiss it.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Storm and Frost

Active

Hold to choose a stance, tap to cast it. Storm opens an eight-second window where every hit you land builds Lightning on your target. Frost grants a budget of softened hits, chilling whoever spends one. Whichever is running also changes what Overcast does.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Overcast

Toggle

A cloud bank erupts around you and follows. Everyone caught inside is blinded and ground down each second, and mobs lose track of their target entirely. From Transcendent it also throws them around. Drains heavily while held.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Nimbus Body

Transcendent FormToggle

Your body comes apart into cloud. Physical damage is halved, blades pass through vapour, though fire and lightning do not care. Weather answers you while it holds.

Weather

Active

Hold to choose a weather, tap to call it. Thunderstorm strikes three times, a second apart. Sunny burns and ignites. Rainy drowns every non-ally around you for four seconds. All three share one cooldown.
