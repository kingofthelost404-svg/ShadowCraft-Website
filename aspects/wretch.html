Wretch

Below Mundane Anti-Utility Aspect Theme · Contempt

You are so wretched that the gods themselves woke up specifically to ruin you. Nothing here helps you. Nothing here was ever going to.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Innate Ability

Held from the moment the Aspect takes hold, outside the rank ladder. Divine Aspects only.

F

Passive

You bleed easy. Anything that lands on you starts hurting immediately and stays twice as long, and a single speck of any buildup fills the whole bar at once.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

U

Passive

You die easy. Everything hits you five times as hard, five times harder again for every Memory you are carrying, and twice again for every rank you were foolish enough to earn. None of those Memories keep their enchantments either.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

C

Passive

You slow fucker. You move and hit at one percent of what you should. Every Memory you carry gives back ten percent, the same Memories that are getting you killed.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

K

Passive

Everyone hates you. Everybody in your cohort or clan carries everything you carry, flaw included, for exactly as long as they are willing to stand near you.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

You

Passive

You don't get one. Y'know what, minus fifty percent to all stats, just for you, pumpkin.
