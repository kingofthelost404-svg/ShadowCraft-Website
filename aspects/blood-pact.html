Blood Pact

Awakened Support Aspect Theme · Blood

An Awakened Support Aspect of Blood: the bearer's blood carries the power to mend life, but every miracle demands a sacrifice of their own vitality, and only a fraction of its power ever works on themselves.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Blood Price

Active

Your blood carries the power to mend life. Tap for a minor cut and a modest mending of whoever you're looking at, ally or enemy alike, or yourself if no one is in your sights. Hold to willingly spill from a far deeper wound: the more severe the sacrifice, the disproportionately stronger the miracle. Every price is a share of your own vitality, so the sacrifice weighs the same at every rank and the miracle grows with you.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Fireheart

Passive

Your heart burns like a furnace, driving your body beyond mortal limits: every blow you land strikes harder and you move faster, extreme heat and cold lose their grip, bleeding sheds quickly, and fresh hot blood burns any attacker who touches you, while healing allies safely, its temperature yours to command. The furnace burns hotter at every rank you climb.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Tenacious Blood

Active

Your blood obeys your will even after leaving your body. Hold to cycle its shape, tap to will that shape into being. The Blade is as deadly as any weapon a soul of your rank could carry. The Shield hardens faster and wider than crafted steel, shrugs off what would break another guard, and tires your footing far less. The Chain seizes whoever you are looking at, holds them fast, leaves them stumbling as they tear loose, and opens a wound where it gripped. The Crystal stores the vitality you spend for it, to be shattered later by whoever holds it. Every shape pays its price in blood.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Scarlet Body

Partial FormToggle

A second heart begins to beat: your physique enlarges slightly, blood production surges, the penitent debt clears twice as fast, and your wounds knit while it holds. Drains essence every second.

Scarlet Salamander

Transcendent FormToggle

Transform into a colossal crimson salamander. Three powerful hearts produce vast amounts of blood, fueling every Blood Pact ability without wounds, while resilience and regeneration soar, but your essence burns away rapidly.
