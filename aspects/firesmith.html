Firesmith

Awakened Utility Aspect Theme · Fire, Forging

An Awakened Utility Aspect for whom fire is not a force but a material. You cannot make fire, you find it, and you work it. Metallic Flame condenses existing flame into real, still-molten metal; Volcanic Storm lays down fuel that something else must light, and burns long enough afterward to feed the forge; the Flaming Anvil makes your best work permanent and forges the eternal flame's own enchantments into it; and at your peak the Magma Sentinel makes your own body the furnace.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Metallic Flame

Active

Grant fire the properties of forged metal. Stand near a real flame and work it into a weapon, slowly; a blade worth wielding is not made in an instant. Creations stay intensely hot, inflicting overheat on every strike, and last longer the higher your Rank. At Awakened you can shape armor plates as well. Tap to begin or abandon the work; hold to change what you are making.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Volcanic Storm

Active

Create volcanic gas and command every flame born from it. The gas spreads fast and does no harm on its own, but it is extremely flammable, and once lit it detonates in a marching wave of explosions and overheat, then keeps burning long enough to feed your Metallic Flame.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Flaming Anvil

Active

Forge items worthy of the eternal flame. Tap to roll a Firesmith enchantment onto what you hold, your own creation keeps it only as long as the creation lasts, while a weapon made by other means also begins slowly burning anyone but you who carries it. Hold to raise a held creation into a permanent Memory. At Transcendent, sneak and tap to graft an enchantment from the Memory in your off-hand onto the permanent Memory in your main hand.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Magma Sentinel

Transcendent FormToggle

Your body becomes black volcanic rock threaded with flowing magma. Its heat ignites Volcanic Storm on its own, so Metallic Flame needs no outside fire at all; your resistances rise, and everything hostile standing close to you slowly cooks.
