Devotee of Demeter

Dormant Utility Aspect Theme · Seasons, Plants, Life

A Utility Aspect of Seasons, Plants and Life, the ancient myth of Demeter fills you with life. Things grow faster where you walk, your herb-lore closes wounds, and what you love can be sealed away safe. Take from you what you love, and the ground itself will be forbidden to grow.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Mother Knows Best

Passive

You hold knowledge over medicinal herbs and the things that bring others life. Plants grow markedly quicker around you, harvesting sometimes turns up an Herb, five of which craft a Healing Solvent that closes wounds a little quicker, and food goes further in you than in anyone else.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Mother's Embrace

Active

Your motherly instincts wish to protect at all costs. A cocoon of ice and plants closes over whoever you are looking at, or over yourself. It always holds its occupant still and blunts almost all harm against them, over you or an ally that is shelter and a shield; over an enemy it is a cage.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Mother's Rage

Active

Losing what was dearest to you has torn you apart, others shall know pain. Every plant within twenty blocks is forbidden to grow and quickly withers away. Anyone who remains inside slowly rots, and in time begins to slow.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Four Seasons

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a giant of all four seasons at once, standing in a field of flowers that will not let your enemies leave. Everything held in the field is sliced by your roses or rotted by your blight, your choice, and the stalks will spring you into the air a second time. Allies pass through untouched. Drains essence every second.

Roses or Rot

Toggle

Choose what the field of flowers does to what it holds: roses that cut and bleed, or a creeping rot that poisons.
