Lifebringer

Awakened Utility Aspect Theme · Support

A Utility Aspect of blessing: healing hands, a soothing voice, and a domain none of your allies escape unblessed, while your own body pays for every grace it gives away.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Blessed Touch

Active

Thankful are those who receive your touch: heals a touched ally for 3 hearts, yet only half that for yourself. The Angelic form doubles both, and delivers the touch remotely to anyone inside your Domain of Life.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Soothing Voice

Active

Your voice gains the ability to soothe all those nearby: strips every harmful effect from yourself and your allies within earshot.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Domain of Life

Active

None within escape your blessings: lays a 10x10 blessed ground at your feet, Strength II and steady regeneration for you and your allies inside, for as long as it holds.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Angelic Form

Transcendent FormToggle

Become an angelic being: doubled Blessed Touch, a wider Domain of Life, and remote touches inside it, but every transformation is bought with a minute of Wither your own abilities cannot cleanse. Drains essence every second.
