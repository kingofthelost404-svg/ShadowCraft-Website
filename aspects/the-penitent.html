The Penitent

Sacred Support Aspect Theme · Atonement

The burden of sin is too heavy for many to carry. It is your place to intercede, to take the crushing weight from your allies' shoulders and bear it yourself, that they may go free.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Penitence

Active

Every blow your comrades strike and every life they take settles on your shoulders. They quicken and mend under the lightened load while you slow, weaken, and finally go blind beneath it. Cast the weight off and it tears through your own body, and half of it through every stranger nearby. Let it fill, and it kills you where you stand.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Redemption's Whispers

Active

Soothing words for the transgressor. Your circle's afflictions are wiped clean, the fevered are cooled, and nearby beasts briefly forget their violence. You have learned to console yourself as well: creeping afflictions take only half their hold on you.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Vessel of Transgressions

Active

Plant a rune-graven cross to bear the weight in your stead. The sins still gather, but your back stays straight while you keep near it, and casting them off costs your body nothing, the vessel shatters instead. Stray too far, or let the burden fill, and the vessel is lost.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Transcendent form still in design. Nothing to document yet.
