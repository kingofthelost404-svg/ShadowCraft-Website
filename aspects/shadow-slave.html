Shadow Slave

Divine Divine Aspect

A Divine aspect of adaptation, growth, imitation, stealth, and eventually authority over shadows. Every advancement pushes the bearer closer to becoming a true Divine Shadow: control shadows, travel through shadows, create shadows, then become shadow itself.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Innate Ability

Held from the moment the Aspect takes hold, outside the rank ladder. Divine Aspects only.

Shadow Bond

Passive

Your Innate Ability: a mystical master-servant contract. Whoever speaks your True Name first becomes your master, and you cannot disobey their direct commands until the bond is broken, but the bond also forges extraordinary trust and synchronization between you.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Shadow Control

Toggle

Your Dormant Aspect Ability: command over shadows as additional limbs, manipulating and extending your own shadow, scouting and hiding through darkness, enhancing objects, and strengthening yourself, your Memories, and your shadow companions.

Later known as [Shadowbinder]

Shadow Sense

Passive

Your Dormant Aspect Ability: every creature casts a shadow, and you have grown attuned enough to read them, nearby creatures are sensed through their own ever-present shadow, not their visible body, letting you find them even sneaking or unseen.

Shadow Wrapping

Toggle

The shadows lying loose around you are not only yours to command, they are yours to wear. Wind them onto your own frame and everything you deal lands harder while everything aimed at you lands softer, both by the same measure. The wrapping deepens as you do, and once you have become shadow enough yourself it costs you nothing at all to hold.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Shadow Step

Active

Your Awakened Aspect Ability: travel through shadows, teleporting between connected shadows, repositioning instantly in battle, turning intangible while inside a shadow, hiding completely from enemies, and covering enormous distances along a connected shadow network.

Later known as [Shadow Stride]

Shadow Passage

Toggle

Unlocked alongside Shadow Step: become a true shadow, your body dissolves into a flat dark shape gliding along the ground. Requires standing in shadow to begin and drops you back to normal the moment you step into the light, lose your footing, or run out of essence. While dissolved, the shadow you are looking at is ringed with sparks and reachable shadows ahead glint, look at one and walk toward it to instantly blink there, essence permitting, chaining freely from shadow to shadow. Pressing again blinks deliberately to the marked shadow at the same cost; sneak+press always reforms.

Shadow Manifestation

Active

Your Awakened Aspect Ability: shape shadow essence into tangible constructs, weapons, spears, chains, armor, wings, walls, platforms, hands, even monsters, real enough to interact physically with the world. The foundation for later techniques like Shadow Shell.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

No ability at this rank.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Divided Mind

Active

Your Transcendent transformation: open the shadow-vessel gaze on your own body and one shadow vessel per formed soul core, each fighting entirely on its own, guarding its post or following you, the moment you aren't inhabiting it. Pick which one to pour your consciousness into next, inspect any of them, or summon one (or all) straight to your side.

Shadow Augmentation

Active

Reabsorb every summoned, uninhabited body into yourself, each one becomes a stacking buff to attack, armor, and speed instead of a separate fighter. Activate again to release them all back into the world.
