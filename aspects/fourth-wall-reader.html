Fourth Wall Reader

Transcendent Combat Aspect Theme · The Fourth Wall

A Transcendent combat Aspect that reads the world like a page, hidden stats laid bare, soul shards invested into permanent power, and literary tricks pulled from a library only you can see.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Protagonist Special

Active

Open the ledger of your own stats and permanently invest soul shards into Health, Damage, Essence Regen, or Resistance. Non-refundable, the page, once annotated, stays annotated.

Sight into the 4th Dimension

Passive

Look at anything and its hidden numbers surface at the edge of your vision, essence, shards, rank, and Soul HP for a person; a rank and class for a creature.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Book Marking

Active

Equip literary modifiers pulled from your library, each one gated by a trivia quiz on the Spell's own world before it takes hold.

Mental Block

Passive

Your own mind resists intrusion, buildup toward Mind and Madness accumulates more slowly.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

4th Wall Inversion

Active

Sneak + right-click a foe to invert a page of their own narrative against them, weakening their stats for an hour.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Demon King of Freedom (Partial)

Partial FormToggle

Half-step into the Demon King's silhouette, every equipped bookmark reads one and a half times as strong.

Demon King of Freedom

Transcendent FormToggle

Fully unveil the Demon King of Freedom, every equipped bookmark doubles in strength, and the Demonic Contract opens to you.

Demonic Contract

Active

Sacrifice equipped bookmarks to forge the Sword of Impurity, the Crown of Hell, or the Spear of Cerberus, only while the Demon King's full form holds.
