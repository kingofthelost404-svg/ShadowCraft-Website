Graviton

Ascended Combat Aspect Theme · Force, Balance

A Combat Aspect of force and balance: every force seeks balance, and you decide where that balance lies. Haul the world in or throw it out, hang an anomaly in the air that bends whatever crosses it, and become a pulsar that swallows blows and hurls them back.

Ability Archive

Every ability this Aspect grants, in the order the soul unlocks them, from Dreamer to Transcended.

Dreamer

Granted the moment the Aspect takes hold.

Mass Shift

Active

Reach out and decide which way the world falls. A burst around you that hauls everything inward, or throws it out with real impact behind it. Hold the key to flip which way the force points.

Awakened

Unlocked at the Awakened soul rank.

Weighted Strike

Active

Give your blows mass. Arms a run of strikes that each hit far harder, drive the target into the ground, stagger them, shrug past part of their armour and wreck the armour on the way through. Both the number of strikes and everything they do grow with your rank.

Ascended

Unlocked at the Ascended soul rank.

Gravity Well

Active

Hang an anomaly where you are looking. In its attracting polarity everything inside moves as though through treacle, shots crossing it bend toward the centre, and climbing away from it is far harder than falling toward it. Inverted, footing becomes unpredictable, a light shove pushes outward, and the ground turns to ice underfoot. You are never caught in your own well, and only one may exist at a time. Hold the key to flip its polarity.

Transcended

Unlocked at the Transcendent soul rank.

Absorption of Force

Transcendent FormToggle

Become a pulsar: rays sweep from above and below you while your body spins. Blows aimed at you are partly swallowed and banked instead of landing. Hold the key to throw everything banked back at what you are looking at as a shockwave. Drains essence every second.
