Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. This policy outlines how ShadowCraft handles and protects any information collected when you use our website, services, or server.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect minimal technical data necessary to operate our server and community. This may include your Minecraft username, Unique Identifier (UUID), IP address, and basic interaction logs within our Discord server or website.

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively to maintain gameplay functionality, process whitelist access, enforce community rules, and protect our server against malicious attacks or unauthorized exploits.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

ShadowCraft does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Data may only be processed through trusted third-party services required for hosting (such as Modrinth, Discord, or server host infrastructure).

4. Cookies & Web Analytics

Our website may use essential browser storage or cookies to remember basic navigation preferences. We do not use intrusive tracking scripts or advertising cookies.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement appropriate technical measures to safeguard your data against loss, unauthorized access, or misuse. However, no internet-based transmission or server environment can be guaranteed 100% secure.
