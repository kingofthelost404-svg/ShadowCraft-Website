Server Rules & Code of Conduct

To ensure a fair and dark-fantasy immersive experience for all players in the Dream Realm, please review and abide by our community regulations.

1. General Conduct & Community

Treat all players and staff members with respect. Toxic behavior, harassment, hate speech, and excessive spam in global chat will not be tolerated.

2. Fair Play

Unfair advantages diminish the survival experience. Use of hacked clients, X-ray texture packs, or unauthorized automation scripts is strictly prohibited.

3. Exploits & Bug Reporting

Exploiting bugs, item duplication glitches, or abusing game mechanics for personal gain is forbidden. Any game-breaking glitches should be reported immediately on our Discord.

4. World & PvP Engagement

Respect zone designations. While PvP and open-world conflict are central to the Nightmare Spell, unprovoked spawn camping or griefing designated safe havens (such as Great Citadels) will result in disciplinary action.
